In 2011, astronomers discovered a massive cloud of water vapor surrounding a quasar
more than **12 billion light-years** away.

The cloud holds at least **140 trillion times** the amount of water in all of Earth's
oceans — roughly 4,000 times the mass of the Sun, all of it in the form of vapor
hundreds of degrees below freezing but heated by the quasar's energy.

It is the largest and oldest reservoir of water ever detected in the universe,
and it proves that water was already widespread when the universe was still young.
