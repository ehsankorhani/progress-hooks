Mercury completes one orbit around the Sun in just **88 Earth days**, but it rotates
on its axis only once every **59 Earth days**.

Thanks to a strange 3:2 spin-orbit resonance, the Sun appears to move very slowly
across Mercury's sky: one full sunrise-to-sunrise cycle takes **176 Earth days** —
which is exactly two Mercury years.

So on Mercury, a single day lasts two of its years. If you lived there, you could
celebrate two birthdays every day. 🎂
